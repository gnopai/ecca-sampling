\begin{frame}{Plan: Get Sampling Areas}
	We will choose sampling areas for the ECCA volunteers to clear and subsequently gather data from.

	Sampling areas should
	\begin{itemize}
		\item Be representative of both on/off ramp areas and the open road
		\item Enable comparison to the 2022 Washington Statewide Litter Study
	\end{itemize}

	We'll get this to ECCA by April 22nd, our next meeting.
\end{frame}

\imageframe{sorting.png}

\begin{frame}{Plan: Generate Measurement Protocol}
	We will design a measurement protocol for the ECCA volunteers.

	This should
	\begin{itemize}
		\item Enable comparison to the 2022 Washington Study
		\item Be compatible with the EPA's Escaped Trash Assessment Protocol
	\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Overview}
	We'll give ECCA a method to measure trash buildup.

	The method will enable comparisons to other data.

	Data is useful if it helps address heavy trash buildup;

	Data may be used to argue for litter reduction efforts.
\end{frame}
